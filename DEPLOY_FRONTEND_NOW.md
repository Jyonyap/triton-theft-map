# 🚀 Deploy Frontend NOW - Ultra Quick Guide

## You're 5 Minutes Away from Going Live!

Your backend is already deployed and working. Let's get your frontend live!

---

## Step 1: Go to Vercel (1 minute)

1. Open: **https://vercel.com/**
2. Click **"Sign Up"**
3. Click **"Continue with GitHub"**
4. Authorize Vercel

---

## Step 2: Import Project (1 minute)

1. Click **"Add New..."** → **"Project"**
2. Find **`triton-theft-map`**
3. Click **"Import"**

---

## Step 3: Configure (2 minutes)

### CRITICAL: Set Root Directory
1. Find **"Root Directory"**
2. Click **"Edit"**
3. Type: `bike-angel-frontend`
4. Click checkmark ✅

### Add Environment Variable
1. Scroll to **"Environment Variables"**
2. Click **"Add"**
3. Fill in:
   - **Name**: `VITE_API_BASE_URL`
   - **Value**: `https://triton-theft-map.onrender.com/api`

---

## Step 4: Deploy! (1 minute)

1. Click the big **"Deploy"** button
2. Wait for the celebration animation 🎉
3. Click **"Visit"** to see your live site!

---

## Step 5: Update Backend CORS (1 minute)

1. Copy your Vercel URL (e.g., `https://triton-theft-map.vercel.app`)
2. Go to: **https://dashboard.render.com**
3. Click your service → **"Environment"** tab
4. Find **`FRONTEND_URL`**
5. Update to your Vercel URL
6. Click **"Save Changes"**

---

## Done! 🎉

Your app is now LIVE at your Vercel URL!

Test it:
- Register an account
- Log in
- View the map
- Report a parking spot
